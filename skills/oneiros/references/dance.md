# Dance

Dance produces video and audio. Ask only for missing shot, motion, duration,
aspect, sound, or reference choices required by the live workflow schema.

Auto may recommend a Fast Proxy or Same-Model Draft. Disclose that a proxy made
with a different model can drift from the final. Guided retains the user's
workflow choice. Manual retains exact supported controls. Experimental routes
must be labeled using live catalogue metadata.

Keep prompts limited to the shot being rendered. Put later cuts, CTA plans, and
delivery notes in the production brief. Preserve original Takes and separate
editable audio or assembly assets. Do not describe diffusion generation as live
streaming or call a draft final.
